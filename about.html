How it began

About us

Founded in 1983, Linetop began by developing software for large computers and offering IT services. By the early 2000s, affordable microprocessors and the reliability of Windows XP enabled compact, battery-powered devices and desktop analytics—paving the way for our first visitor counters.

What happend next

We saw the opportunity and built battery-powered data loggers for indoor and outdoor use, perfect for sites without mains power. These counters stored visitor data on removable flash cubes, later uploaded to a PC. The portable system was simple, reliable, and widely adopted.

We improved our sensors and launched a Windows analytics app, followed by USB and radio systems sending counts from park entrances to nearby PCs . Later, IoT telemetry enabled hourly or daily uploads via mobile networks to Linetop’s servers, with data accessible through a browser dashboard offering charts, tables, exports, and reports.

Today, our systems also support LoRa and Wi-Fi connectivity, giving clients flexible options for real-time data access and remote monitoring.

• our mission

🔹 Turning Data into Insight

Our PC app and online dashboard do more than count visitors—they deliver actionable insights. By analyzing peak hours, dwell times, and movement patterns, organizations can improve efficiency, enhance visitor experience, and plan smarter strategies.
