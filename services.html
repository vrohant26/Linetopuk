Our Services

Comprehensive Visitor Counter Services – From Planning to Reporting

At Linetop, we provide end-to-end services to ensure your visitor counters are installed, maintained, and delivering accurate data. Whether you need one counter or a network across multiple sites, our team supports you at every stage— planning, installation, training, and reporting.

Data Collection, Analysis & Report Writing

Our training enables client staff to manage counters independently. However, some projects prefer to contract ongoing visitor monitoring to Linetop. In Wales, for example, we manage counters across government forestry and nature reserves, providing:

  • Regular data collection and inspection.
  • Maintenance and servicing.
  • Full visitor data reports.

Other operational models include:

  • Client-led maintenance: Staff replace batteries, swap memory cubes, and send them to a regional centre for combined reports.
  • Postal submissions: Staff mail used cubes to Linetop for analysis—ideal for teams focused on fieldwork

Digital Sharing: Clients can email counter data to Linetop. Each run creates a small hourly file compiled by EcoPC into site-year reports. Sharing is easy—just email the files. We review results, flag anomalies, and assist with issues.

Professional Installation & Staff Training

Installation is often carried out by Linetop staff, followed by a handover session or later review once data is available. During installation, your staff can act as helpers to learn hands-on skills, making them capable of:

  • Installing additional counters in the future.
  • Moving existing counters to new sites.
  • Handling routine repairs with spare parts sent by post.

We also offer demonstration installs, where our team sets up a few counters while training your staff to install the remainder.

Vehicle Counter Installations: Installing counters in roads or car parks requires technical setup and safety planning. Costs are lower if client staff handle traffic. All road works, including inductive loops, are two-person tasks..

Pre-Sales Support & Project Planning

Linetop provides free online pre-sales support to help you design a visitor monitoring system that meets your goals. For projects of any size, we:

  • Provide illustrated proposals (methods statements) outlining equipment, installation plans, and training requirements.
  • Work with digital photos from your site, adding sketches and ideas to refine the best solution.
  • Request key site details such as dimensions of posts, fencing, or railings to ensure custom boxes and enclosures fit perfectly.
  • Advise on paint colours (RAL codes) to blend counters seamlessly into their environment.

For new buildings, we recommend planning counters at the design stage, especially for complex entrances or high-traffic doors.

On-site Assessments: For larger or regional projects, our team (or UK, Ireland, and EU agents) can visit, meet staff, and take accurate measurements. Charges depend on time and travel from South Wales.
