Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 11/11/2025

Linetop is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. What We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, or phone number submitted via contact forms or direct communication
  • Client data: Visitor count data transmitted from Linetop counters to our secure cloud platform

We do not collect or track:

  • Website usage data (e.g., pages visited, time spent, browser/device details)
  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to inquiries or support requests
  • Provide access to your counter data via our cloud platform
  • Fulfill contractual or service obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share data only with:

  • Trusted service providers who support our operations (e.g., cloud hosting)
  • Legal authorities if required by law

4. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data, including secure servers and access controls.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: service@linetop.co.uk

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
